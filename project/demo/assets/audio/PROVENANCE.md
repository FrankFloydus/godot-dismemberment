# Demo audio provenance

All recordings below are available under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- **Shotgun fire.** Four separate near-miked discharges recorded by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney. The first two clips come from the Benelli Nova recording O_21P.wav. The other two come from the Winchester Model 12 recording K_22P.wav. Each clip uses one close channel at 48 kHz mono, with the attack trimmed tightly, low-mid energy reinforced from that same recording, and the reflection tail attenuated. [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library)
- **Footsteps.** Four hiking boot steps on stone recorded by Fission9, using steps 1, 3, 4, and 6 from the public high-quality preview. The one-shots were trimmed, lightly filtered for low-frequency rumble, and peak-matched as 48 kHz mono WAV files. [Hiking Boot Footsteps on Stone](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/521590/)
- **Flesh impacts and severing.** The three hit variants are game foley by EZduzziteh, from squishsplat_impact.mp3, squish_02.mp3, and squish_01_0.mp3. The three sever variants use Wet Break 1.wav, Wet Break 4.wav, and Wet Break 9.wav by Zane Little Music. These designed effects were trimmed, filtered, and peak-matched as 48 kHz mono WAV files. [Squish Sounds Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/squish-sounds-effects) [Fleshy Bone Break/Snap SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/fleshy-bone-breaksnap-sfx)
- **Range room tone.** A refrigeration drone recorded by LEGIT Audio, from the CC0 WAV samples in The Shop. The selected recording, TheShopCollection_convenience_store_drinks_fridge_drone_2.wav, was filtered, attenuated, and crossfaded to make a quiet stereo loop. [The Shop](https://opengameart.org/content/the-shop)
