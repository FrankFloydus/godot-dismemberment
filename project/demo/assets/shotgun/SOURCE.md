# Bullpup Shotgun, Fitted

The first-person shotgun mesh in the Kinetic Range demo derives from [Bullpup Shotgun, Fitted](https://3dassets.dev/assets/mega-weapon-pack-bullpup-shotgun-loadout-0f97efcb) by 3D Assets. The publisher identifies the model as AI-assisted and releases it under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The [original GLB](https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/33446/v1/model.glb) has SHA-256 `f6171495de43ad6129dd53cebf0900c6a22828b2b53f2da321a4e7a1294a580f`.

The demo derivative merges the static mesh, adds UVs and baked albedo and roughness maps for its metal, polymer and rubber surfaces, and adjusts the sight materials for the demo lighting. The GLB embeds its textures.
